# L3 Foundation Programme – IIT Sri Lanka

This repository contains academic work and learning materials from the **L3 Foundation Programme** completed at **Informatics Institute of Technology (IIT), Sri Lanka**.

The Foundation Programme is designed to prepare students for undergraduate studies by building strong fundamentals in **computing, mathematics, programming, academic writing, communication, and digital skills**.

---

## 📘 Programme Overview

- Programme Level: L3 (Foundation)
- Institution: Informatics Institute of Technology (IIT)
- Intake: January & May 2023
- Focus: Academic readiness, computing fundamentals, and problem-solving skills

---

## 📚 Modules Covered

### 🔹 General & Academic Skills
- **DOC000 – General Information & Policies**
- **DOC310 – Essential Communication Skills**
- **DOC311 – Academic Skills for Higher Education**

- **Self Assessment Aptitude Test (SAAT)**

### 🔹 Computing & Digital Foundations
- **DOC314 – Digital Skills for Higher Education**
- **DOC327 – Working with Data**
- **DOC330 – Designing Innovative Solutions**

### 🔹 Mathematics & Logic

- **DOC325 – Digital Circuits and Logic Design**

### 🔹 Programming Fundamentals
- **DOC333 – Introduction to Programming Principles**
- **DOC334 – Computer Programming**
